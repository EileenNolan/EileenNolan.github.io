About Me

Hello!

I am a third-year computer science Ph.D. student at Brown University. My research focuses on privacy-preserving cryptography, where I aim to develop technologies that enhance online privacy and address the growing vulnerabilities of our digital lives. I am very grateful to be advised by Dr. Anna Lysyanskaya.

My current research interests include:

  • Anonymous tokens with hidden metadata
  • Anonymous credentials and mercurial signatures
  • Private set intersection and heavy hitters
  • Cryptographic image authentication
